Typography Hierarchy

Role Typeface Sizes & Line Height Application
Display / Brand Playfair Display 48/56px, 36/44px, 30/36px Hero titles, founder quotes, section headlines. Infuses classical South Indian literary warmth.
Functional UI Inter 18/28px, 16/24px, 14/20px Body copy, admissions form fields, campus contact pills. Maximizes legibility on mobile screens.
Tracking & System JetBrains Mono 12/16px, 11/14px Admissions reference passes (e.g., KL-2026-SRI-402) in the Staff Admin Cockpit.

Touch Target Standards (Fitts’s Law)

  • Primary Action Buttons: Strictly minimum 48px height to prevent mis-taps on mobile devices.
  • Floating WhatsApp Counselor Pill: 56px height, pinned to the lower right thumb zone.
  • Form Inputs: Minimum 48px height with visible 2px focus ring for accessible parent typing.

Section 4 · UX Laws & Engineering

4. The 12 Laws of UX Applied with Empathy

UX Law Empathetic Implementation in Kalalayaa
1. Fitts’s Law All CTAs min 48px. WhatsApp floating action is 56px in the natural thumb arc.
2. Hick’s Law Campus strip has max 7 items; admissions form uses a 2-click intelligent matcher tool.
3. Miller’s Law Never present more than 6 campuses at once without clear thematic grouping.
4. Jakob’s Law Logo top-left, CTA top-right, standard form labels above inputs (no floating labels).
5. Law of Proximity Counselor name, direct phone, and WhatsApp button always co-located in a single bounded pill.
6. Law of Common Region Every campus card is visually bounded with a dedicated color accent bar.
7. Aesthetic-Usability Effect Logo-extracted colors and clean typography build subconscious trust with anxious parents.
8. Peak-End Rule The success modal names the counselor and provides an instant one-tap WhatsApp link.
9. Doherty Threshold Zero heavy JavaScript frameworks; instantaneous <50ms response on all transitions.
10. Von Restorff Effect The active campus chip gets a prominent Deep Teal fill, standing apart from the strip.
11. Zeigarnik Effect Numbered 3-step admissions journey gives parents clarity on the end-to-end path.
12. Serial Position Effect Flagship K.K. Nagar is positioned first; the distinctive E-Techno CBSE School is anchored last.
